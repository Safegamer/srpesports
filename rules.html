Competition format

Four stages to the title

Stage 1 · Grove League60 matches default

Amazon Grove (Group A) and Sundarbans Grove (Group B). The default season blueprint has 24 teams, five matches per team and four matches per day across 15 days.

Stage 2 · Canopy Knockout16 → 8

Sixteen qualifiers compete in eight best-of-three series, with the default plan scheduling two series per day over four days.

Stage 3 · Banyan & Pine Playoffs8 teams

Eight teams are split into Banyan (ranks 1–4) and Pine (ranks 5–8), then progress through semifinals and group finals.

Stage 4 · Evergreen Grand FinalBO3

The Banyan and Pine group champions meet in a best-of-three title match.

Standings & integrity

How the table is calculated

Points: +3 for a win, +1 for a draw and 0 for a loss.

Round difference (RD): the team’s scored rounds minus conceded rounds across recorded matches.

Ranking: points first, then RD, then team name as the stable final ordering.

Disqualification: a disqualified team is moved below eligible teams and visibly marked in the standings. The admin-published reason is shown where available.

Official results: only scoreboards published to the tournament database count toward the public standings and player statistics.

Proof / VOD: administrators may attach a match proof or VOD link to an official result.

Match-day instructions

Room ID, password, match start time, map selection and any match-specific instructions are communicated through official SRP channels. Do not rely on unofficial messages.
