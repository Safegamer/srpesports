SAVE TREES

Season 6 brings a green theme to the SRP-ESPORTS arena. The campaign page is a place to share practical environmental habits alongside the competition.

This page describes the season theme. It does not claim a verified tree-planting total, donation, charity partnership or environmental certification.

Go digital

Use digital schedules, scoreboards and announcements instead of printing copies that quickly become outdated.

Reduce waste

Reuse, repair and recycle everyday items where local collection facilities are available.

Grow responsibly

Support native plants and local green spaces, and follow local guidance when planting or caring for trees.

A small community pledge

Make the green theme mean something

Choose one practical action that you can continue beyond tournament week. Share it with your squad and invite teammates to take part.

  • Keep match announcements and team documents digital.
  • Avoid single-use items where a reusable option is practical.
  • Choose locally appropriate plants and care for them after planting.
